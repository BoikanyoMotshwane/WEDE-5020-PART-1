Website Goals and Objectives:

Website Traffic: 

Specifically for this website, the main aim is to drive consumer products towards the younger demographic for devices and upgrade of older devices to new devices for online purchase and the older generations for mainly electronics  

Insurance and Guarantee 

The Organisation will ensure that the products and service provided will be on warranty and discounts will be applied once the consumer has purchased a product or service

Generate leads 

This is the most valuable point needed is to gain the consumer information by attracting a complimentary service to the end consumer by including social media links to the website’s page or even running ads with in social media platforms or work with influencers.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Website Traffic Increase of 250+ visitors per month 
Increase the product and service sales to 50+ sales per month 
Increase the customer’s engagement on the website for more than 5+ minutes

Key Features and Functionality 


The Main Features for this Website must include:

Home- Everything Else from the options that are mentioned below the home page (Including My Cart, Account)
Products- Core Products being offered (Laptops, Smartwatch, Phones, TVs)
Repairs-All kind of Repairs 
Services Offered-Software Upgrades, Device Upgrades, Accessories
About US – Contacts, History, Special Offers
